ABOUT THE TOURNAMENT

The League System

IPL is a two-day event that finds and builds student teams the way a sports league does. Round one tests aptitude and group discussion. The top 48 move forward, 8 become team captains, and a live auction turns 40 shortlisted participants into 8 mentored teams. Each team then has one day to solve a real industry problem and pitch it to judges.

01

Player Valuation Index (PVI)

Base bidding prices [20–100 points] are dynamically determined by each qualifier's composite performance in the Round 1 Aptitude Test and Group Discussion.

02

Purse Reserve Guardrail

An algorithmic safeguard ensures captains cannot overbid on initial candidates. A mandatory reserve is locked to guarantee sufficient funds to fill all 5 drafted roster slots.

03

Guaranteed Draft Protocol

Every one of the 40 qualifiers is guaranteed to be drafted. Unsold candidates from initial rounds enter accelerated secondary rounds until all 8 squads of 6 are complete.
